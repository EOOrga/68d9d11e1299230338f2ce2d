Four daily controls

The app keeps operational records on the device and presents the core work as focused screens for quick review and action.

Resource allocation

Track opportunity stages, values, owners, and the next action needed to keep work moving.

Visitor desk

Register expected visitors, check arrivals in, and keep host coverage clear throughout the day.

Private by design

The Android app does not require accounts, cloud sync, analytics, network APIs, or Android permission requests. Teams can use it as a lightweight local control room for operational decisions.

Each screen is designed around a practical workflow: create a plan, follow a lead, move training forward, check a visitor in, and review local workspace settings.
